# QI 启 brand

**Name:** QI — from the Chinese 启 (qǐ): to open, to launch, to awaken (as in 启动, "activate").
**Tagline:** Launch a token. Awaken its agent. / 发行代币，启动它的智能。
**Platform token:** $QI

## Logo

`public/qi-symbol.svg` (transparent mark) and `public/favicon.svg` (mark on a dark tile).
A geometric 启: the 户 "door" radical drawn in jade strokes, opening onto a gold sun (the 口) rising over a cut horizon line — the door opens, the agent wakes.
